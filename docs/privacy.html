Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This policy explains how the piano app "Rossum Grand Piano" (the "App") handles information. The App is published by [Emarovic] ("I", "me").

Short version

The App does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

What stays on your device

Your settings (sound, theme, volume and similar choices), saved recordings, imported MIDI songs and your learn-mode stars are stored only on your device, in the App's local storage. I cannot see them. Uninstalling the App deletes them.

Files you choose

If you import a MIDI file, it is read on your device only and is not uploaded anywhere. If you share a recording, the App hands the file to the sharing app you pick; I do not receive it.

Internet use

The Android app includes its sounds and fonts, so it works offline. The web version, or an app that is missing some sound files, may download instrument sound files from public servers (GitHub Pages: tonejs.github.io and gleitz.github.io). Those servers may see your IP address and basic request details under their own policies. The App sends them no personal information.

Permissions

The App does not ask for access to your microphone, camera, contacts, location or storage.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Credits and licences

Piano sounds: Salamander Grand Piano by Alexander Holm (CC BY 3.0). Other instrument sounds: FluidR3 GM SoundFont via the midi-js-soundfonts project by Michael Deal (CC BY 3.0). Built-in songs are traditional or public-domain compositions.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
