## Short description (max 80 characters)

## Full description (max 4000 characters)
Play a beautiful grand piano anywhere, with real recorded sounds and a keyboard built for fast fingers.

PLAY
- Real grand piano recordings, plus electric piano, organ, strings, guitar, harp, vibraphone, choir, violin, flute, bass and more
- 61 keys with zoom: show as few as 5 keys or as many as 21
- Two-row keyboard for both hands, with a sustain pedal you hold with your thumb
- Slide your finger across the keys for glissandos
- Split keyboard, layer two sounds, transpose, tone control and reverb
- Chord names appear as you play
- Works offline

LEARN
- Falling-note lessons that wait for you to play the right note
- Built-in songs, and import your own MIDI files
- Earn stars for accuracy

CREATE
- Record your playing, sustain pedal included
- Play it back, save songs, export a MIDI file or share your recording as audio
- Metronome with time signatures and a count-in, plus drum rhythms

LOOK
- Five colour themes, including a dark Midnight theme
- Performance mode for older phones

No ads. No account. No data collected.

Credits: Piano sounds by Alexander Holm (Salamander Grand Piano, CC BY 3.0). Other instruments: FluidR3 GM SoundFont via midi-js-soundfonts (CC BY 3.0).
